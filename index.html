Wedding Ring

One love. One story. One ring.

MB Jewellery focuses on wedding rings, couple rings or even your own ring concept. We work with you to craft a ring customized to you and your significant other's personality.

Love is special and mysterious. It represents a unique story between a couple. The Couple Collection offers three different engraving styles—Fingerprint, Signature and Memento—to make it unique and memorable.

Fingerprint

Your unique fingerprint engraved on both rings, symbolizing your commitment for life—you are the one and only one.

Signature

Sign your name in your own handwriting, declaring that “You are mine.”

Memento

Write down your words—sweet and memorable moments will always stay in your heart.

Crafted with advanced technology, GIA certified diamonds and three engraving methods, each ring reveals individuality and a lasting commitment to one another.
